# 🎲 Pig Game

A fun, two-player dice game built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Roll the dice, collect points, and decide when to hold your score to avoid losing your current points!

## 🚀 Live Demo

Coming soon!

## 🎮 How to Play

- **Roll Dice:** Roll the dice to add points to your current score.
- **Hold:** Save your current score to your total score and pass the turn to the other player.
- **Roll a 1:** Lose all points accumulated during your current turn, and the next player takes their turn.
- **Win the Game:** The first player to reach 100 points wins.
- **New Game:** Start a new game at any time.
